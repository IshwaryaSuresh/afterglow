Delete Your Account

Afterglow, by MadeForHumans

You can permanently delete your Afterglow account and all associated data at any time. Nothing is held back and nothing is sold.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Afterglow and sign in.
  2. Go to Menu → Account & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete everything and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately, along with everything below.

Prefer we do it for you?

Email ishwaryasuresh97@gmail.com from the address on your Afterglow account with the subject “Delete my account.” We will verify it is you and permanently delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (email, name).
  • Every glow, word, note, and warmth you have shared or written, including your private notes.
  • Your pairing and the shared story (the deletion also unpairs you, so your former partner's copy of the shared story is removed too).
  • Your notification token, if reminders were enabled.

What is kept

Nothing personal is retained. We keep no shadow copies and no analytics profile. Your subscription purchase records are held by the app store (Google Play or Apple), not by us, and are governed by their policies. This deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
